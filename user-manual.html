Overview

What the app does

A student who wants to leave campus raises an outpass request. It travels through a fixed chain — warden, then guardian — and only becomes a usable pass once both have signed off. The gate scans the student out and back in, and the app records both.

On this screen

  • A strip saying On campus or Currently out, with your curfew window.
  • Your latest request, its status pill and what is holding it up.
  • New outpass request — the quickest way in.
  • Counters for Approved, Pending and Rejected, across your whole history.
  • Recent requests, with View all for the rest.
  • One request, one trailCategory, destination, reason, times, emergency contact and any supporting document travel together. Nothing is decided over a phone call the record does not show.
  • Two approvals, in orderThe warden clears it first, then the guardian decides. Neither can be skipped, and both are timestamped against the pass.
  • Everyone hears immediatelyEach step pushes a notification to the people it affects and leaves a copy in the in-app inbox, so turning push off never loses the record.
  • Late returns are loggedIf the gate scans a student in after the time on their pass, the difference is recorded with the reason and what the office did about it.
  • Presence, when enabledGuardians see whether their ward is on campus from the gate scans, and — if the student opts in — from a live location fix as well.

Getting started

Signing in for the first time

There is no sign-up screen. Accounts are created by the hostel office, and the office issues your first password. Everything below happens once.

On this screen

  • Your email address — the one on your campus record.
  • Your password, with an eye icon to check what you typed.
  • Forgot password — emails a reset link to the address on record.
  • An Institution ID field appears only if your address exists at more than one institution.

Sign in

  1. Open the app

    If you have signed in before, the app restores your session and goes straight to your dashboard.

  2. Enter your email address

    The address on your campus record. The app remembers it for next time.

  3. Enter your password

    Tap the eye icon to check what you typed. Forgotten it? Forgot password emails a reset link to the address on record.

  4. Tap Continue

    The server decides your role and the app opens the matching dashboard. You never pick student, guardian or warden yourself.

Asked for an Institution ID? That field only appears when the same email or number exists at more than one institution. Enter the ID the campus office gave you and sign in again.

Set your own password

If you are still on the password the office issued, the app takes you straight to Change password and will not go anywhere else until you have set one. There is no back button — the only way out is a new password or signing out.

  • At least 8 characters, checked before anything is sent.
  • Type it twice; the second field has to match.
  • Afterwards you land on whatever your account needs next — linking, or your dashboard.

Connect your account to your campus record

A brand-new account is not yet attached to a student record. The app shows a One more step screen:

Students — roll number

The roll number on your ID card, exactly as the office has it. That links your account to your record — room, department, year, site and guardians all come with it.

Guardians — mobile number

The mobile number the campus holds for you. One entry links every ward that number appears against, so a parent with two children on campus gets both in a single step.

"Not on the roster" means the office has not added it yet, or it was typed differently. "Already connected to another account" means somebody has claimed it — your warden can sort that out. Wardens skip this screen entirely.

Staying signed in

  • Your session is held in the phone's secure keystore and restored each time you open the app.
  • It refreshes quietly in the background, so a long session never interrupts you.
  • Signed out unexpectedly? The sign-in screen says why — your session timed out, or the server ended it (signed in elsewhere, or an administrator closed it).
  • Profile → Sign out also switches push notifications off for that device.
The flow

The approval chain

Every pass follows the same path. Knowing where a request sits tells you who is holding it up — and the status pill says exactly that, in words.

1 · Warden first

A new request sits at With warden until the site warden clears it. Nothing reaches the guardian before that.

2 · Guardian second

Once cleared it moves to Awaiting guardian. They approve, refuse, or ask to speak to the warden first.

3 · Then the gate

The warden activates the approved pass. The student is scanned out, scanned back in, and it closes as Completed.

Requests that leave the chain do so in one of five ways:

  • Rejected by warden — turned down before the guardian ever sees it.
  • Rejected by guardian — turned down with a reason the student is shown.
  • Escalated — the guardian did not answer in time, so the warden is asked to step in.
  • Cancelled — pulled back by the student before the pass went live.
  • Expired — the window passed without the pass being used.

There is no QR code pass. The app does not draw a scannable code. The gate looks the student up and records the scan against the pass, which is what puts Scanned out and Scanned in on the record.

Student guide

Your home screen

Four tabs — Home, Request, History, Profile — and everything about where you stand is on the first one.

  • Where the gate last saw youA strip at the top reads On campus or Currently out, with your site's curfew window beside it.
  • Latest requestYour request in flight, with its status pill and a line saying what is holding it up. Tap it for the full timeline.
  • Approved · Pending · RejectedThree counters across your whole history, not just what is on screen.
  • Late returns on recordThe warden reviews these when clearing your next pass, so it is worth seeing before you ask again.

Raising an outpass request

On this screen

  • Your name and roll number, filled in from your record.
  • Category chips, each with its own rules underneath.
  • Leaving and Returning date and time pickers.
  • Destination, Reason and an emergency contact.
  • An optional — sometimes required — supporting document.
  1. Check the summary

    Name, roll number, room and site come from your record. If any of it is wrong, fix it with a profile change request first.

  2. Pick a category

    The chips come from your campus. Each carries its own rules, shown underneath: a maximum duration, and whether a supporting document is required.

  3. Set when you are leaving

    Tap the Date and Time boxes under Leaving. They default to right now.

  4. Set when you are coming back

    Defaults to four hours later. The app prints how long you will be away, and turns it red if the return is before the departure or the trip is longer than the category allows.

  5. Destination and reason

    The reason is what your guardian reads before deciding, so it is worth a full sentence.

  6. Emergency contact

    A number you can actually be reached on. Yours is pre-filled.

  7. Attach a document if needed

    PDF or image, up to 5 MB. Optional for most categories — a category marked Required will not submit without one.

  8. Submit

    Goes to your warden, and notifies your guardian at once. You land back on Home with the new request at the top.

Submit button greyed out? A field is empty, the return is at or before the departure, the trip is over the category maximum, or a required document is missing. The screen says which — look for the red note.

History and tracking

The list, and one pass

  • Chips filter by state; the magnifying glass searches reason, destination and category.
  • Each row carries the pass ID, category, reason, dates and destination.
  • Open one for the full record and the timeline — one row per stage, marked done, current or pending.
  • Cancel request sits at the bottom while the request can still be pulled back.

History holds every request you have raised. Filter with the chips — All, Pending, Approved, Active, Rejected, Expired, Cancelled — or tap the magnifying glass to search by reason, destination or category.

Tap any request to open it. The detail screen shows:

  • The pass ID and status pill.
  • Category, destination, leaving and returning times, emergency contact, when you raised it.
  • Scanned out and Scanned in times, once the gate has recorded them.
  • Any note the warden or your guardian left with their decision.
  • A timeline of the chain — one row per stage, marked done, current, pending or rejected.

Cancelling

Cancel request appears while the request can still be pulled back — a draft, with the warden, cleared by the warden, awaiting your guardian, or approved but not yet activated. Once a pass is active it cannot be cancelled from the app, and a cancellation cannot be undone.

Profile and location sharing

Your Profile lists roll number, room, site, phone, email and home address. It is read-only — see requesting a change. Underneath sits one control that is yours alone: Location sharing. When it is on —

  • Only the guardians on your record can see it — not other students, not the hostel at large, and not the warden.
  • Your position is sent at most once a minute, and only while the app is open. Nothing goes in the background.
  • What your guardian sees is whether you are inside or outside the campus boundary, and how old the reading is.
  • Turn it off and nothing further is sent. What was already sent stays on your record.

Location sharing is off until you turn it on, and leaving it off does not affect your passes — your guardian still sees the gate scans either way.

Guardian guide

Approvals

The Approvals tab is the front door: everything waiting on your decision, with the buttons on the card. The tab badge counts requests across all your wards, so a second child's request never goes unseen.

On this screen

  • A note counting what is waiting on you.
  • The student, how long ago they asked, and the full reason — never truncated.
  • Leaving and returning times, destination and their contact number.
  • Reject and Approve right on the card, plus Talk to the warden first.

More than one child on campus?

You see one ward at a time. The only sign of that is a small caret next to the name in the header — tap the name to switch, and the queue re-points at that child. When a sibling has something waiting, a note tells you and points at the switch. A guardian with one ward gets no caret at all.

Making a decision

Each card carries the student's name and roll number, how long ago they asked, the pass ID and category, the full reason — never truncated — and the leaving and returning times.

Approve

Records it at once. The student and the warden are both told, and the pass moves on to be activated.

Reject

Asks for a reason. What you type is shown to the student, so it is worth a sentence rather than a word.

Talk to the warden first

Not a decision. It alerts the warden that you want to discuss this before deciding, and they will call you. The request stays open.

Two guardians on one student? The first response counts. Tap a button after the other guardian has answered — or after you replied over WhatsApp — and the app says Already decided and refreshes rather than showing an error. Repeating the same decision is always harmless.

Your ward

On this screen

  • One card reconciling the last gate scan with the live location fix, when there is one.
  • Passes this term and Late returns — the second is a link to the full log.
  • Hostel details: room, block, site and the warden responsible.
  • Call warden, Message student and Report an emergency.

The Ward tab is everything about one child in one place.

  • Where they areOne card reconciles two sources: the last gate scan (in or out, which gate, when) and — if your ward has switched on location sharing — a live fix saying whether they are inside the campus boundary and how fresh the reading is.
  • Passes this term · Late returnsTwo counters. The second is a link — tap it for the full late-entry log: what time the pass expected them, when the gate actually scanned them in, the gap, the reason, and what the office did.
  • Guardians on recordEveryone the campus lists against this student, with relationship, phone number, and which one is you.
  • Hostel detailsRoom, block, site, and the name and phone number of the warden responsible — plus Call warden and Message student.

Raising an emergency alert

Ward → Report an emergency puts an alert in front of every warden on your ward's site, immediately, with a push notification.

  1. Pick a category

    Medical, Family, Safety or Other.

  2. Say what has happened

    Your own words go straight onto the warden's dashboard, unclipped. This is what they act on.

  3. Give a callback number

    Shown beside the alert so a warden can reach you without hunting for your record.

  4. Tap Alert the warden

    It sits at the top of every warden's overview until one of them marks it resolved.

One alert per 30 seconds. If you need to add something, wait a moment — or use Call warden on the same screen.

Warden guide

Overview

The warden's Overview tab is a live picture of the sites assigned to you: what is waiting, who is out, and anything that has gone wrong.

On this screen

  • Open emergency alerts above everything else, each with Resolve.
  • Four counters: Pending, Approved today, Currently out, Overdue.
  • Quick tools — Passes, Profiles, Announce, Inbox.
  • A running activity feed of scans, decisions and overrides.
  • Open emergency alerts, above everything else — category, student, the guardian's own message and a callback number, each with a Resolve action.
  • Four counters: Pending Approved today Currently out Overdue — across your assigned sites, not just the page in front of you.
  • Quick tools: Passes, Profile edits, Announce, Inbox.
  • Recent activity: gate scans, decisions, cancellations, overrides, announcements and profile edits, each with how long ago.

The pass queue

On this screen

  • Every pass across your sites, newest first, with the student named on each row.
  • Chips filter by state; the search box covers roll number, pass ID, name, reason and destination.
  • The download icon exports exactly what the filter shows, as CSV.
  • Chips filter by state: All, Pending, Approved, Active, Rejected, Expired, Cancelled.
  • Search covers roll number, pass ID, student name, reason and destination — one box for every way you might look for a pass.
  • Filtering and searching happen server-side, so a long history is never downloaded just to be narrowed.

Clearing, activating and rejecting

Tap a request to open it. You get the same detail every role sees, plus the actions that are yours. An action only appears when it is actually this request's next step.

ActionAppears whenWhat it does
Clear for guardian approval Status is With warden or Escalated Records your approval and starts the guardian flow. They are notified at once.
Activate pass Status is Approved Makes the pass live so the gate can let the student out. Status becomes Active.
They agreed / They refused Status is Escalated or Contacting guardian Logs a guardian's answer that came in over the phone rather than in the app.
Override status Always Forces the pass into a chosen state, bypassing the chain. Requires a note, and is written to the audit log.

How do I reject a request? Use Override status and pick Reject, then give the reason. The note is what the student sees, and the override is recorded against your account. The same sheet offers Approve on the guardian's behalf, Mark active, Mark completed, Cancel and Mark expired.

Escalated requests

A request is Escalated when the guardian never responded in the app. Call them, ask for a decision, then tap They agreed or They refused — it is logged against the pass as the guardian's decision, noted as having come in out of band.

Profile edits, announcements and export

Profile change requests

Students and guardians cannot edit their own records, so every correction arrives here. The Profiles tab badges how many are pending; each shows the field, current value, requested value, reason and any attached proof. Approve writes it to the record and notifies the requester.

Announcements

Overview → Announce sends a titled message to Everyone, Students, Guardians or Wardens across your sites — in-app inbox and push.

Exporting passes

The download icon in the Passes title bar exports whatever the chips and search box currently show, as CSV, into your phone's share sheet. Capped at 5,000 rows, so narrow the filter first for a big range.

Warden vs. administrator. A warden works a single site's queue. Organisation-wide settings — student groups, app branding and changing anybody's role — belong to the administrator and do not appear in the warden's app at all.

Notifications

The bell in every dashboard header opens your inbox, badged with what you have not read. Tapping a notification takes you straight to the pass it is about — whether you tap it in the inbox or on your lock screen.

Students

  • Warden cleared or turned down your pass
  • Guardian approved or refused it
  • Your pass is active
  • Scanned out / back on campus
  • Profile change decided

Guardians

  • Approval needed — waiting on you
  • The warden's decision
  • Your ward scanned out or back in
  • A late return was recorded
  • Pass activated, expired or cancelled

Wardens

  • New pass request for review
  • A guardian has responded
  • A guardian wants to speak to you
  • A request has escalated
  • Emergency alert from a guardian

Profile → Notifications has separate switches for Push, Email and SMS. Turning push off stops the alert, not the record — the in-app inbox still fills.

Everyone

Account & settings

The Profile tab looks much the same for all three roles: who you are, your details, then settings.

Requesting a change to your details

Students and guardians cannot edit their own record — a wrong phone number or room is corrected by the office, so the roster stays the single source of truth. What you can do is ask.

  1. Profile → Request an update

    Lists only the fields you may change, each showing what is currently on record.

  2. Tick the fields

    Type the new value beside the current one. Several at once is fine.

  3. Say why

    And attach proof if you have any — PDF or image up to 5 MB.

  4. Submit

    The state of your request shows on your profile, so you never file the same one twice.

One open request at a time. If you already have one pending, the screen shows it instead of a blank form.

The rest of the settings

  • Notifications — push, email and SMS switches.
  • Change password — at least 8 characters, typed twice.
  • Language — whichever languages your campus has enabled.
  • Help & support — your campus's support email or phone number.
  • Terms & privacy — the legal documents for your institution.
  • Sign out — ends the session and stops push on this device.
Everyone

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the things people ask most.

?I do not have an account. How do I sign up?

You cannot — and that is deliberate. Accounts are created by the hostel office and come with a first password. If sign-in says your account was not found, contact the office rather than trying another address.

?Sign-in is asking for an Institution ID.

Your email or number exists at more than one institution, so the app cannot tell which account you mean. Enter the ID the campus office gave you. The field only appears when it is actually needed.

?I have forgotten my password.

Tap Forgot password and enter your email address. A reset link goes to the address on your campus record. If that address is out of date, only the office can change it.

?The app signed me out on its own.

The sign-in screen says which of the two happened. Your session timed out is routine. This device was signed out by the server usually means the account signed in somewhere else, or an administrator ended the session.

?My room number or phone number is wrong. Can I fix it?

Not directly. Profile → Request an update, tick the field, type the correct value and say why. A warden or administrator applies it, and you are notified when they do.

?Why will my request not submit?

One of four things, and the form says which in red: a required field is empty; the return time is at or before the departure; the trip is longer than that category's maximum; or the category requires a supporting document and none is attached.

?Can I cancel a request after submitting it?

Yes — while it is a draft, with the warden, cleared by the warden, awaiting your guardian, or approved but not yet activated. Once the pass is active it cannot be cancelled from the app, and a cancellation cannot be undone.

?Both of my parents got the request. Who decides?

Whoever answers first. If the other guardian has already decided, the app says Already decided and refreshes instead of showing an error. Sending the same decision twice is always harmless.

?What does "Talk to the warden first" actually do?

It is not an approval or a refusal. It alerts the warden that you want to discuss the request before deciding, and they will call you. The request stays open and still needs your answer afterwards.

?I have two children on campus. Where is the second one?

Behind the ward switcher — tap your child's name in the header. When a sibling has something waiting, the dashboard says so, and the tab badge always counts every ward on your account.

?Why can I not see where my ward is?

Live location is opt-in and belongs to the student: they turn it on from Profile → Location sharing, and it only reports while their app is open. Some campuses have not enabled it. Either way you still see gate scans.

?Who can see a student's location?

Only the guardians listed on that student's record, and only while the student has sharing switched on. Not other students, not the wider hostel, and not the warden. Position is sent at most once a minute and never in the background.

?Warden: how do I reject a request?

Override status → Reject, then write the reason. The note goes to the student, and the override is recorded in the audit log against your account.

?Warden: what does "Activate pass" mean?

An approved pass is not yet a live pass. Activating it flips the status to Active, which is what tells the gate the student may leave. It only appears once the guardian has approved.

?Warden: a request says "Escalated". What now?

The guardian never answered in the app. Call them, ask for a decision, then tap They agreed or They refused. It is logged as the guardian's decision, noted as having come in out of band.

?Is there a QR code to show at the gate?

No. The app does not draw a scannable pass. The gate looks the student up and records the scan against the pass, which is what puts Scanned out and Scanned in on the record.

?Can I stop the notifications?

Profile → Notifications has separate switches for push, email and SMS. Turning push off stops alerts on your device; the in-app inbox still records everything.

?Which phones does the app run on?

Android 7.0 and newer, and iPhones on iOS 15.1 and newer. It works on tablets too.

?Why does my app show a different name and icon?

Institutions can brand the app for a hostel block or group, so students and guardians in that group see their own name, colour and mark. Everything in this manual works identically underneath.

Help

Still stuck?

Take the fastest route for what you need.

Something about your record

Wrong roll number, room, guardian or phone number, or an account that will not link — the hostel office owns the roster.

Something about a pass

A request stuck at a stage, a decision to discuss, a late return you disagree with — your site warden. Their number is on the guardian's Ward screen.

Something about the app

Crashes, missing screens or a button that does nothing — Profile → Help & support opens the contact your campus configured.
